# 🔍 RFQ vs PO - KEY DIFFERENCES

**Date:** January 2025  
**Purpose:** Clear explanation of the differences between RFQ (Request for Quotation) and PO (Purchase Order)

---

## 🎯 **QUICK SUMMARY**

| Aspect | RFQ (Request for Quotation) | PO (Purchase Order) |
|--------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **Full Name** | Request for Quotation | Purchase Order |
| **When** | BEFORE vendor selection | AFTER vendor selection |
| **Purpose** | Get quotes from vendors | Confirm purchase with vendor |
| **Sent To** | MULTIPLE vendors | ONE vendor |
| **Contains Prices?** | ❌ NO (vendors provide prices) | ✅ YES (agreed prices) |
| **Stage in Process** | Step 3 (competitive bidding) | Optional step (after selection) |
| **Status** | 📥 Asking for quotes | 📤 Confirming purchase |
| **Vendor Response** | Vendor submits quotation | Vendor acknowledges PO |
| **Accounting Impact** | ❌ None | ❌ None |

---

## 📋 **DETAILED COMPARISON**

### **1. WHAT IS RFQ?**

**RFQ (Request for Quotation)** is a document you send to **MULTIPLE vendors** to ask them to **provide quotes/prices** for goods or services you need.

**Think of RFQ as:**
- 🎯 A request: "Please tell us how much you'll charge"
- 📢 Competitive bidding: Multiple vendors compete
- 🔍 Price discovery: Find the best price
- ✅ Before decision: You haven't chosen a vendor yet

**Key Characteristics:**
- ✅ Sent to **MULTIPLE vendors** (usually 3-5 vendors)
- ✅ **NO prices** - vendors provide prices in their quotes
- ✅ **Competitive process** - vendors bid against each other
- ✅ **Before vendor selection** - helps you choose the best vendor
- ✅ **NO commitment** - you're just asking for quotes

---

### **2. WHAT IS PO?**

**PO (Purchase Order)** is a formal document you send to **ONE vendor** to **confirm your purchase commitment** after you've selected them.

**Think of PO as:**
- ✅ A confirmation: "Yes, we want to buy from you"
- 📝 Formal contract: Agreement to purchase
- 💰 Price already agreed: Based on accepted quotation
- ✅ After decision: You've already chosen the vendor

**Key Characteristics:**
- ✅ Sent to **ONE vendor** (the winning vendor)
- ✅ **WITH prices** - contains agreed prices from quotation
- ✅ **Confirmation document** - formalizes the purchase
- ✅ **After vendor selection** - you've already chosen them
- ✅ **Commitment** - you're committing to purchase

---

## 🔄 **IN THE PROCUREMENT WORKFLOW**

### **RFQ IN THE FLOW:**

```
1. Create Requisition (list items needed)
   ↓
2. Approve Requisition
   ↓
3. ⭐ CREATE RFQ → Send to MULTIPLE vendors
   ├─ Vendor A receives RFQ
   ├─ Vendor B receives RFQ
   ├─ Vendor C receives RFQ
   └─ All vendors review your requirements
   ↓
4. 📥 VENDORS SUBMIT QUOTATIONS
   ├─ Vendor A: $5,000
   ├─ Vendor B: $4,500 ← Best price!
   └─ Vendor C: $5,200
   ↓
5. 📊 REVIEW QUOTATIONS → Select Vendor B (best price)
   ↓
6. ✅ ACCEPT QUOTATION from Vendor B
   ↓
7. [OPTIONAL: Create PO → Send to Vendor B]
   OR
   ✅ Create Invoice directly from quotation
```

**RFQ Position:** Step 3 - Competitive bidding phase

---

### **PO IN THE FLOW (OPTIONAL):**

```
1. Create Requisition
   ↓
2. Approve Requisition
   ↓
3. Create RFQ → Send to MULTIPLE vendors
   ↓
4. Vendors submit quotations
   ↓
5. Review quotations → Select winning vendor
   ↓
6. Accept winning quotation
   ↓
7. [OPTIONAL] ⭐ CREATE PO → Send to ONE vendor (winning vendor)
   ├─ Contains agreed prices from quotation
   ├─ Vendor receives PO
   └─ Vendor acknowledges receipt
   ↓
8. Vendor delivers goods/services
   ↓
9. Vendor sends invoice
   ↓
10. Approve invoice → Payment
```

**PO Position:** Step 7 - Optional confirmation step (can be skipped)

## 📊 **SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON**

### **WHEN TO USE**

| RFQ | PO |
|-----|-----|
| ✅ **Before** vendor selection | ✅ **After** vendor selection |
| ✅ To get competitive quotes | ✅ To confirm purchase |
| ✅ To find best price | ✅ To formalize agreement |
| ✅ For new purchases | ✅ After price agreement |
| ✅ When prices unknown | ✅ When prices agreed |

---

### **WHO GETS IT**

| RFQ | PO |
|-----|-----|
| 📢 **MULTIPLE vendors** (3-5 vendors) | 📧 **ONE vendor** (winning vendor) |
| All vendors you want quotes from | Only the vendor you selected |
| Competitive bidding | Single vendor commitment |

---

### **WHAT IT CONTAINS**

| RFQ | PO |
|-----|-----|
| ❌ **NO prices** | ✅ **WITH prices** |
| Items/services needed | Items/services to purchase |
| Quantities required | Quantities to purchase |
| Specifications | Specifications |
| Delivery requirements | Delivery terms (agreed) |
| Deadline for quotes | Payment terms (agreed) |
| **Vendors fill in prices** | **Prices already agreed** |

---

### **PURPOSE**

| RFQ | PO |
|-----|-----|
| 🎯 **Ask for quotes** | ✅ **Confirm purchase** |
| 📥 Request information | 📤 Send confirmation |
| 🔍 Price discovery | 💰 Price commitment |
| 🏆 Vendor selection | 🤝 Vendor agreement |

---

### **VENDOR RESPONSE**

| RFQ | PO |
|-----|-----|
| 📥 **Vendor submits quotation** | ✅ **Vendor acknowledges PO** |
| Vendor provides prices | Vendor confirms receipt |
| Vendor competes with others | Vendor prepares delivery |
| Multiple quotations received | Single acknowledgment |

---

### **ACCOUNTING IMPACT**

| RFQ | PO |
|-----|-----|
| ❌ **NO accounting entries** | ❌ **NO accounting entries** |
| No expense recorded | No expense recorded |
| No liability created | No liability created |
| No cash affected | No cash affected |

**Note:** Accounting entries are created when you **PAY the invoice**, not when creating RFQ or PO!

## 💡 **REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE**

### **Scenario: Buying Office Supplies**

#### **STEP 1: CREATE RFQ** 📢

You need 100 office chairs. You create an RFQ and send it to 5 vendors:
- Vendor A (Furniture Store A)
- Vendor B (Furniture Store B)
- Vendor C (Furniture Store C)
- Vendor D (Furniture Store D)
- Vendor E (Furniture Store E)

**RFQ Says:**
> "We need 100 office chairs. Please provide your best quote including delivery."

**No prices in RFQ** - vendors will provide prices.

---

#### **STEP 2: VENDORS SUBMIT QUOTATIONS** 📥

Vendors respond with their quotes:
- Vendor A: $50 per chair = **$5,000 total**
- Vendor B: $45 per chair = **$4,500 total** ← Best price!
- Vendor C: $55 per chair = **$5,500 total**
- Vendor D: $52 per chair = **$5,200 total**
- Vendor E: $48 per chair = **$4,800 total**

---

#### **STEP 3: SELECT VENDOR B** ✅

You review all quotations and select **Vendor B** because they have the best price ($4,500).

---

#### **STEP 4A: CREATE PO (OPTIONAL)** 📝

**If you want a PO:**
You create a PO and send it to **Vendor B only**.

**PO Says:**
> "We confirm our purchase of 100 office chairs at $45 per chair = $4,500 total. Please deliver by [date]."

**PO contains the agreed price** ($4,500).

---

#### **STEP 4B: CREATE INVOICE DIRECTLY (SKIP PO)** 💰

**If you skip PO:**
The system creates an invoice directly from Vendor B's quotation.

**Invoice created with:**
- 100 office chairs
- $45 per chair
- $4,500 total

---

#### **STEP 5: VENDOR DELIVERS & INVOICES** 🚚

Vendor B delivers the chairs and sends you an invoice for $4,500.

---

#### **STEP 6: PAY VENDOR** 💳

You approve and pay the invoice. **NOW** accounting entries are created!

## 🎯 **KEY TAKEAWAYS**

### **RFQ:**
1. ✅ Sent to **MULTIPLE vendors** before selection
2. ✅ **NO prices** - vendors provide quotes
3. ✅ Purpose: **Competitive bidding** and price discovery
4. ✅ **No commitment** - just asking for quotes
5. ✅ Essential step in RFQ-based procurement

---

### **PO:**
1. ✅ Sent to **ONE vendor** after selection
2. ✅ **WITH prices** - contains agreed prices
3. ✅ Purpose: **Confirm purchase** commitment
4. ✅ **Commitment** - formal agreement to purchase
5. ✅ **Optional** in RFQ-based procurement

## 🤔 **WHEN TO USE PO vs SKIP PO**

### **✅ USE PO - When:**

1. **Formal Documentation Needed:**
   - Large/complex purchases
   - Legal/compliance requirements
   - Audit trail requirements

2. **Delivery Tracking:**
   - Need to track deliveries against PO
   - Invoice matching against PO
   - Quality control process

3. **Multi-Stage Deliveries:**
   - Goods delivered in multiple shipments
   - Partial delivery tracking
   - Receipt verification

---

### **✅ SKIP PO - When:**

1. **Simple Purchases:**
   - Small/straightforward orders
   - Services (no physical delivery)
   - Recurring purchases

2. **RFQ-Based Flow:**
   - Competitive bidding already done
   - Quotation acceptance = commitment
   - Invoice created directly from quotation

3. **Quick Processing:**
   - Need faster workflow
   - Reduce paperwork
   - Streamline process

**Your System Recommendation:** ✅ Skip PO for simplicity - invoice is created directly from quotation!
