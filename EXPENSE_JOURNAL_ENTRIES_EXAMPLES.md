# Complete Expense Journal Entries Examples

## Overview

This document provides comprehensive examples of journal entries for all expense categories under different payment scenarios (Cash, Bank, Credit). Each example shows the complete double-entry bookkeeping with proper account mappings.

## Expense Categories & Account Mappings

| Category | Account Number | Account Name | Description |
|----------|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Salaries and Wages** | 5000 | Salaries and Wages | Employee compensation |
| **Travel and Accommodation** | 5030 | Travel and Entertainment | Business travel expenses |
| **Operations** | 5090 | Other Operating Expenses | General operational costs |
| **Branding and Marketing** | 5040 | Marketing and PR | Marketing and advertising |
| **Office Equipment** | 5020 | Office Equipment | Office supplies and equipment |

## Payment Method Account Mappings

| Payment Method | Account Number | Account Name | Description |
|----------------|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| **CASH** | 1000 | Cash and Cash Equivalents | Petty cash payments |
| **BANK** | 1000 | Cash and Cash Equivalents | Bank account payments |
| **CREDIT** | 2000 | Accounts Payable | Credit/liability payments |

## Complete Examples by Category

### 1. SALARIES AND WAGES EXPENSES

#### Scenario 1.1: Salaries Paid via Bank Transfer (Taxable)
**Transaction**: Monthly payroll of $8,000 (15% VAT)

**Journal Entry**:
```
Dr. Salaries and Wages (5000)     $8,000.00
Dr. VAT Input (1101)              $1,200.00
    Cr. Bank Account (1000)        $9,200.00
```

**Explanation**:
- Debit: Salaries expense increases (P&L impact)
- Debit: VAT Input increases (asset - can be claimed back)
- Credit: Bank account decreases (cash outflow)

#### Scenario 1.2: Salaries Paid via Cash (Non-Taxable)
**Transaction**: Petty cash salary advance of $500 (no VAT)

**Journal Entry**:
```
Dr. Salaries and Wages (5000)     $500.00
    Cr. Cash Account (1000)        $500.00
```

**Explanation**:
- Debit: Salaries expense increases
- Credit: Cash decreases (no VAT for non-taxable)

#### Scenario 1.3: Salaries on Credit (Taxable)
**Transaction**: Accrued salaries of $6,000 (15% VAT)

**Journal Entry**:
```
Dr. Salaries and Wages (5000)     $6,000.00
Dr. VAT Input (1101)              $900.00
    Cr. Accounts Payable (2000)    $6,900.00
```

**Explanation**:
- Debit: Salaries expense increases
- Debit: VAT Input increases (asset)
- Credit: Accounts Payable increases (liability - money owed)

## VAT Treatment Summary

### Taxable Expenses (15% VAT)
- **VAT Input Account (1101)**: Always debited for the VAT amount
- **Total Payment**: Base amount + VAT amount
- **VAT Reclaimable**: Yes, can be claimed back from tax authorities

### Non-Taxable Expenses (0% VAT)
- **No VAT Input Account**: No VAT account involved
- **Total Payment**: Base amount only
- **VAT Reclaimable**: No, cannot claim VAT back

---

## Account Impact Summary

### Income Statement Impact (P&L)
- **Expense Accounts (5000s)**: Increase expenses, reduce profit
- **VAT Input (1101)**: Reduces net VAT liability

### Balance Sheet Impact
- **Assets**: Cash/Bank decreases, VAT Input increases
- **Liabilities**: Accounts Payable increases (for credit transactions)

### Cash Flow Impact
- **Cash/Bank Payments**: Immediate cash outflow
- **Credit Transactions**: No immediate cash impact, future liability

## Verification Checklist

### ✅ For Each Expense Created:
1. **Correct Expense Account**: Verify the right 5000s account is debited
2. **VAT Treatment**: Check if VAT Input is debited (taxable) or not (non-taxable)
3. **Payment Account**: Confirm correct payment account is credited
4. **Balanced Entry**: Ensure debits = credits
5. **Amount Accuracy**: Verify all amounts are correct

### ✅ System Validation:
1. **Category Mapping**: Test all 5 categories map to correct accounts
2. **Payment Methods**: Test CASH, BANK, and CREDIT scenarios
3. **VAT Scenarios**: Test both taxable and non-taxable expenses
4. **Journal Entry Generation**: Verify proper double-entry bookkeeping

This comprehensive system ensures accurate financial reporting and proper expense categorization for all business scenarios! 🎯
