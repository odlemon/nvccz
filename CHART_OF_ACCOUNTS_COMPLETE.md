## 🎯 **OVERVIEW**

The Chart of Accounts is the foundation of the financial management system, providing a structured framework for recording, classifying, and reporting all financial transactions. This comprehensive chart supports double-entry bookkeeping and enables accurate financial reporting across all business operations.

---

## 🏗️ **ACCOUNT STRUCTURE**

### **Account Numbering System**
- **1000s**: Assets (Current, Fixed, Long-Term, Contra-Assets)
- **2000s**: Liabilities (Current, Long-Term)
- **3000s**: Equity (Share Capital, Retained Earnings)
- **4000s**: Revenue (Income from operations)
- **5000s**: Operating Expenses
- **6000s**: Investment-Related Accounts
- **VAT Accounts**: Tax compliance and tracking

### **Account Types**
- **Current Asset**: Short-term assets (cash, receivables)
- **Fixed Asset**: Long-term assets (equipment, property)
- **Long-Term Asset**: Investments and long-term holdings
- **Contra-Asset**: Accumulated depreciation, allowances
- **Current Liability**: Short-term debts and obligations
- **Long-Term Liability**: Long-term debts and obligations
- **Equity**: Owner's equity and retained earnings
- **Revenue**: Income from business operations
- **Expense**: Operating costs and expenses
- **Income**: Investment and other income

### **Financial Statements**
- **Balance Sheet**: Assets, Liabilities, Equity
- **Income Statement**: Revenue, Expenses, Net Income
- **Cash Flow**: Cash movements and tracking
- **Income Statement / Cash Flow**: Combined reporting
