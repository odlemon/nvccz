Traditional procurement processes involve:
- Manual requisition and approval processes
- Phone calls and emails to collect vendor quotes
- Spreadsheet-based quote comparisons
- Manual invoice processing
- Disconnected accounting entries
- Limited audit trails

### 2.2 Solution

The Procurement Module provides an integrated system where:
- Employees create requisitions online
- System automatically sends RFQs to vendors
- Vendors submit quotes online instantly
- System compares quotes automatically
- Approval workflows route documents to right people
- Invoices and payments create accounting entries automatically

## 4. PROCUREMENT WORKFLOW

### 4.1 Complete Workflow (Step-by-Step)

#### STAGE 1: Purchase Requisition

**Step 1.1: Employee Creates Requisition**
- Employee identifies items/services needed
- Fills out online form with:
  - Title (e.g., "Office Supplies - January 2025")
  - Description
  - Department
  - Priority level
  - Justification
  - List of items with quantities
- No prices or vendors needed at this stage
- Saves as draft, can edit before submitting

**Step 1.2: Employee Submits for Approval**
- Reviews requisition
- Clicks "Submit for Approval"
- Can no longer edit
- Status: Pending Approval

**Step 1.3: Manager Reviews and Approves**
- Department Head or Deputy receives notification
- Reviews requisition details
- Checks justification
- Adds comments if needed
- Approves or rejects
- Employee receives notification of decision

**Step 1.4: If Approved**
- Status: Approved
- Ready for RFQ creation
- Procurement team notified

---

#### STAGE 2: Request for Quote (RFQ)

**Step 2.1: Create RFQ**
- Procurement team creates RFQ from approved requisition
- Selects multiple vendors (minimum 2, recommended 3+)
- Sets quotation deadline
- Specifies delivery requirements
- Adds any special terms

**Step 2.2: System Sends RFQ to Vendors**
- Professional email sent to all selected vendors
- Email includes:
  - RFQ number and date
  - Items/services required
  - Quantities and specifications
  - Deadline for submission
  - Big "Submit Quotation Online" button
- Each vendor gets unique submission link

**Step 2.3: Track Vendor Responses**
- System shows which vendors:
  - Received email
  - Viewed RFQ
  - Submitted quotation
  - Haven't responded

---

#### STAGE 3: Vendor Quotations

**Step 3.1: Vendor Receives RFQ Email**
- Vendor clicks "Submit Quotation Online" button
- Opens quotation form (no login needed)
- Form is mobile-friendly

**Step 3.2: Vendor Submits Quotation**
- Vendor fills in:
  - Company details
  - Contact information
  - Price for each item
  - Payment terms (e.g., Net 30)
  - Delivery time
  - Quote validity period
  - Any special notes
- System calculates totals automatically
- Vendor clicks submit

**Step 3.3: Vendor Receives Confirmation**
- Confirmation email sent immediately
- Includes quotation number for reference
- Procurement team notified of new quote

**Step 3.4: Multiple Vendors Submit**
- Process repeats for each vendor
- System collects all quotes
- Ready for comparison

---

#### STAGE 4: Quotation Review and Selection

**Step 4.1: Review All Quotations**
- Procurement team views all submitted quotes
- System displays comparison table:
  - Side-by-side price comparison
  - Payment terms comparison
  - Delivery time comparison
  - Item-by-item breakdown
- Lowest prices highlighted automatically

**Step 4.2: Select Winning Quotation**
- Team evaluates based on:
  - Price
  - Delivery time
  - Payment terms
  - Vendor reliability
  - Past performance
- Selects best overall value
- Adds selection notes/justification

**Step 4.3: Accept Winning Quotation**
- Clicks "Accept Quotation"
- Adds acceptance notes
- System automatically:
  - Creates invoice from quotation
  - Sets invoice status to Draft
  - Sends congratulations email to vendor
  - Records selection in audit trail

**Step 4.4: Reject Other Quotations**
- For each non-winning quote:
  - Clicks "Reject Quotation"
  - Provides professional reason
  - System sends respectful rejection email
  - Thanks vendor for participation
  - Encourages future submissions

---

#### STAGE 5: Invoice Approval

**Step 5.1: Invoice Auto-Created**
- System creates invoice automatically
- All data from accepted quotation
- Status: Draft
- Ready for approval workflow

**Step 5.2: Submit Invoice for Approval**
- Procurement submits invoice
- System identifies approval workflow
- Example for Finance Department:
  - Step 1: Accountant reviews
  - Step 2: Finance Manager approves

**Step 5.3: First Approval (Accountant)**
- Accountant receives notification
- Reviews invoice details
- Verifies amounts match quotation
- Checks all required information
- Approves or rejects with comments
- If approved, moves to next step

**Step 5.4: Second Approval (Finance Manager)**
- Finance Manager receives notification
- Reviews invoice and first approval
- Gives final approval or rejects
- Adds comments as needed

**Step 5.5: Invoice Fully Approved**
- Status changes to Approved
- System creates accounting entry:
  - Records expense
  - Records VAT (if applicable)
  - Creates liability (Accounts Payable)
- Entry balanced and posted immediately
- Ready for payment

---

#### STAGE 6: Payment Processing

**Step 6.1: Finance Processes Payment**
- Finance team opens approved invoice
- Clicks "Process Payment"
- Enters payment details:
  - Payment method (Bank Transfer, Cash, Check)
  - Payment reference number
  - Payment date
  - Bank account used

**Step 6.2: System Records Payment**
- Creates accounting entry:
  - Clears liability (Accounts Payable)
  - Reduces cash/bank balance
- Invoice status: Paid
- Payment timestamp recorded

**Step 6.3: Vendor Notification**
- System sends payment confirmation email
- Includes:
  - Payment amount
  - Payment reference number
  - Payment date
  - Invoice details
  - Thank you message

---

#### STAGE 7: Cycle Complete

**Final Status:**
- Requisition: Approved
- RFQ: Completed
- Quotation: Accepted (winner) / Rejected (others)
- Invoice: Paid
- Vendor: Notified
- Accounting: All entries posted
- Audit Trail: Complete

## 7. BUSINESS RULES

### 7.1 Requisition Rules

1. Requisitions do NOT include prices or vendor selections
2. Only draft requisitions can be edited
3. Only Department Head or Deputy can approve requisitions from their department
4. Requisition must be approved before creating RFQ
5. Each requisition gets unique number (REQ-YYYYMM-XXXX)

### 7.2 RFQ Rules

6. RFQ must be sent to minimum 2 vendors (recommended 3+)
7. RFQ must include items from approved requisition
8. Each RFQ gets unique number (RFQ-YYYYMM-XXXX)
9. Vendors must submit quotations before deadline
10. Each vendor receives unique submission link

### 7.3 Quotation Rules

11. Vendor quotation submission does NOT require login or account
12. Only ONE quotation per RFQ can be accepted
13. Accepting quotation automatically creates invoice
14. Rejected quotations must include reason
15. Acceptance/rejection sends email to vendor

### 7.4 Invoice Rules

16. Invoice from accepted quotation includes all quotation data
17. Invoice approval follows department-specific configuration
18. Invoice must go through all configured approval steps
19. Invoice approval creates accounting entry:
    - Records expense
    - Records VAT (if applicable)
    - Creates liability (Accounts Payable)
20. Only approved invoices can be paid
21. Payment creates accounting entry:
    - Clears liability
    - Reduces cash/bank balance
22. Payment amount must match invoice total
23. Payment sends confirmation email to vendor

### 7.5 Approval Rules

24. Only one active approval configuration per department
25. Configuration defines both requisition and invoice approval
26. Approval steps are executed in order (sequential)
27. User's system role determines if they can approve invoices
28. Finance department has 2-step invoice approval:
    - Step 1: Accountant review
    - Step 2: Finance Manager final approval

### 7.6 Accounting Rules

29. All accounting entries must be balanced (debits = credits)
30. Entries are posted immediately when created
31. Invoice approval entry links to invoice
32. Payment entry links to invoice
33. Each entry gets unique number (JE-YYYYMM-XXXX)

### 7.7 Notification Rules

34. RFQ email includes "Submit Quotation Online" button
35. Acceptance email congratulates vendor
36. Rejection email is professional and encouraging
37. Payment confirmation includes payment reference
38. All emails are logged for audit

## 11. GLOSSARY

**Accounts Payable:** Money your company owes to vendors for goods/services received

**Approval Configuration:** Rules that define who must approve requisitions and invoices for each department

**Chart of Accounts:** List of all accounts used to record financial transactions

**Department Head:** Manager in charge of a department, can approve requisitions

**Department Role:** User's position in their department (Head, Deputy, or Member)

**Deputy Head:** Second-in-command in department, can also approve requisitions

**Journal Entry:** Accounting record showing debits and credits for a transaction

**Member:** Regular employee who can create requisitions but not approve them

**Purchase Requisition:** Internal request to purchase goods or services

**RFQ (Request for Quote):** Document sent to vendors asking for price quotes

**System Role:** User's role for invoice approvals (e.g., Finance Manager, Accountant)

**Vendor Quotation:** Price quote submitted by vendor in response to RFQ
